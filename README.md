# Pawwp: The Social Hub for Pets & Their Humans

Pawwp is a specialized social media platform designed for pets, shelters, and animal lovers. It combines the visual engagement of Instagram with the urgent community focus of Twitter, all tailored to the welfare and celebration of animals.

## Core Concepts

### Top Catches (48-Hour Feed)
The main feed is ephemeral. Media posted here stays for only **48 hours**, keeping the community vibrant and current.

### Pet Profiles & 9-Slot Showcase Reel
Every pet has a dedicated profile. While the global feed is fast-moving, pet owners can "Showcase" up to **9 selected posts** on the profile indefinitely. It's the pet's highlight reel!

### Account Types
- **Personal**: For pet parents to share the lives of their companion pets.
- **Shelter/NGO**: For organizations to manage multiple animals, facilitate adoptions, broadcast via Telegram-style channels, and raise funds.

### Interaction Language
- **Boops**: Our version of "Likes". Because every pet deserves a nose boop.
- **Woofs**: Our version of "Comments". Start a conversation.

## Key Features

### Telegram-Style Shelter Channels & Town Square
Shelters operate official broadcast channels where designated administrators broadcast adoption requests, emergency rescue calls, and fundraisers. These updates stream into the global Town Square civic board and can be browsed individually per shelter.

### Town Hall Q&A
A community advice hub where pet parents can ask urgent health questions (e.g., toxic food ingestion like chocolate), dietary guidance, and training tips, receiving verified answers from shelters and veterinarians.

### DM Centre
Direct messaging to facilitate adoptions, coordinate rescues, or chat with fellow pet lovers.

## Tech Stack
- **Backend**: Go (Golang) with standard library routing.
- **Frontend**: React 18, Vite.
- **Database**: PostgreSQL with GORM.
- **Auth**: JWT with Access & Refresh token rotation.
- **Storage**: (Planned) Google Drive integration for media.
