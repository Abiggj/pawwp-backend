# 🐾 Pawwp: The Social Hub for Pets & Their Humans

Pawwp is a specialized social media platform designed for pets, shelters, and animal lovers. It combines the visual engagement of Instagram with the urgent community focus of Twitter, all tailored to the welfare and celebration of animals.

## 🌟 Core Concepts

### 📸 The 48-Hour Feed
The main "FYP" (For You Page) is ephemeral. Media posted here stays for only **48 hours**, keeping the community vibrant and current.

### 🏆 Pet Profiles & Showcase
Every pet has a dedicated profile. While the global feed is fast-moving, pet owners can "Showcase" up to **9 selected posts** on the profile indefinitely. It's the pet's highlight reel!

### 🐕 Account Types
- **Personal**: For pet parents to share the lives of their furry, feathered, or scaled companions.
- **Shelter/NGO**: For organizations to manage multiple animals, facilitate adoptions, and raise funds.

### 🐾 Interaction Language
- **Boops**: Our version of "Likes". Because every pet deserves a nose boop.
- **Woofs**: Our version of "Comments". Start a conversation.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🚨 SOS Page
A dedicated space for emergency situations. NGOs can list themselves as verified channels, and users can post urgent alerts (lost pets, medical emergencies, etc.) to get immediate community help.

### 📢 Adoption & Donation Feed (The "Town Square")
A Twitter-style text-heavy feed specifically for:
- Adoption calls.
- Fundraising for medical bills or shelter supplies.
- Community announcements and pet advice.

### 💬 DM Centre
Direct messaging to facilitate adoptions, coordinate rescues, or just chat with fellow pet lovers.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Backend**: Go (Golang) with standard library routing.
- **Database**: PostgreSQL with GORM.
- **Auth**: JWT with Access & Refresh token rotation.
- **Storage**: (Planned) Google Drive integration for media.
